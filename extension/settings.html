Settings

Recipes you share from your iPhone with the Send to Recipe to Cart shortcut wait in your Google inbox, then show up on the Recipes page.

iPhone inbox

From the Google Apps Script setup (see docs/iphone-shortcut.md).

When a recipe arrives

Recipes arrive as links, so the extension needs permission to read recipe sites. Chrome asks once when you save.

Checking for new recipes

Chrome checks when it starts and every few minutes while it's open.
