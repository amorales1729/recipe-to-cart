Settings

Recipes you share from your iPhone with the Send to Recipe to Cart shortcut wait in your Google inbox, then show up on the Recipes page.

iPhone inbox

From the Google Apps Script setup (see docs/iphone-shortcut.md).

When a recipe arrives

Recipes arrive as links, so the extension needs permission to read recipe sites. Chrome asks once when you save.

Instant pickup and phone alerts

Uses ntfy.sh, a free push service with no account. Your inbox pings a private channel the moment a recipe arrives, so Chrome starts right away instead of at the next check. In Fill my Walmart cart right away mode, your phone also gets a note when the cart is ready or Walmart needs you. The pings never include your recipes. Save after turning it on or off.

Keep it private: anyone with the name can read the alerts.

Checking for new recipes

Chrome checks when it starts and every 5 minutes while it's running, as a backup to instant pickup.
